How classification works: 1D and 1H are checked separately using 20/50-period moving averages and recent price range. Uptrend and downtrend require both 1D and 1H to match. A signal appears when a coin newly enters Uptrend and BOTH 1D and 1H are classified as uptrend. RSI 55 crossing is not required. Ranging does not require a specific timeframe: a coin is listed if the scanner identifies ranging conditions in either checked interval. The ranging page hides the timeframe columns. This is a technical filter, not a guarantee or a buy/sell recommendation.
Data note: This standalone page requests public market data from Binance's public data endpoint when opened with an internet connection. It scans every active Spot USDT pair returned by Binance; scanning all pairs may take longer and can be affected by network/API rate limits.
